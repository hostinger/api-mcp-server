# headless — Build mode "Bring your own frontend" (design)

Read from [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) when `brief.md` → **Build mode** is **Bring your own frontend**.

The user drives the design — **do not generate three directions or write a prescriptive `design.md` over their project.**
The frontend already exists (or the user vibe-codes it with you); your job is to follow *their*
direction, not impose one. Skip `SKILL.md` Steps 1–7, `keep-look.md` included, even when
**Design direction** keeps the look of the app in this folder: their own code already carries it.
Either write nothing, or capture only what the user explicitly asks for (a short note of their
stated colours/vibe) so the frontend step has it, then do only the `state.json` update at the end of `SKILL.md` Step 7 so the flow
moves on to frontend.
